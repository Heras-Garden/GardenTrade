# Commands

Command-based management remains available for administration and organization shops.

- `/shop create <quantity> <price>`
- `/shop companycreate <company> <quantity> <price>`
- `/shop governmentcreate <government> <quantity> <price>`
- `/shop info`
- `/shop buy [units]`
- `/shop delete`
- `/shop list`
- `/shop companylist <company>`
- `/shop governmentlist <government>`

## Sign shop setup

Attach a wall sign to the stock container while holding the item to sell.

- Line 1: `[Shop]`
- Line 2: quantity
- Line 3: `B <price>`
- Line 4: optional

GardenTrade rewrites the sign with the seller, quantity, price, and item name.
