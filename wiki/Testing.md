# Testing

2. Confirm the floating item and price display appears.
3. Buy once with a second player.
4. Confirm stock decreases by the exact amount and Obols move once.
5. Test insufficient stock.
6. Test insufficient buyer balance.
7. Test a full buyer inventory.
8. Restart and confirm the sign shop and visual return.
9. Delete the shop and confirm its floating displays are removed.
10. Test a company or government shop and confirm revenue goes to the correct principal.
